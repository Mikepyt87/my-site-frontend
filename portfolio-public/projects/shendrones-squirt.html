Another aircraft, another arrangement

The June 2022 post describes moving from a ProTek25 to a slammed HD Shendrones Squirt. This aircraft is kept separate from the LEGO-themed freestyle build.

A separate cinewhoop build

I assembled this Shendrones Squirt around a GoPro Bones setup and integrated the commercial FPV components into the ducted frame. The propeller ducts, camera placement, wiring, and available space all had to work together inside the same aircraft. I keep this page separate from the Player1 and LEGO-themed builds because the frame, camera setup, and intended arrangement were different, even though the general FPV skills carried across all three.

Credits & project notes +

Assembly: Michael Pytlowany. Shendrones supplied the underlying aircraft design; camera and electronics are commercial components.
