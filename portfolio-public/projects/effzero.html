Start with the whole machine

This is a separate machine from my Voron V0/V0.2 build. The two 250 mm Voron 2.4s are separate machines again, not alternate names for this printer.

The April 2025 post shows this machine running its homing sequence, including probe attachment and detachment. The finished build and the parts collection belong to the same story: a working assembly supported by specific modifications.

The original caption reports 20,000 mm/s² acceleration. The clip is a record of that homing routine, not a print-quality or acceleration benchmark.

The details live underneath

The FZero folder contains two M8P mounting-deck variants, a 45 mm mezzanine spacer, an extended foot-riser block, skirts and trim, and a TFT-to-skirt bracket. These are the less visible details around the controller, base, and display.

They are presented as modifications within an existing printer ecosystem, not as authorship of the entire printer design. The separately named imported folder remains distinct from the shared modification files.

A build that can be explored in more than one way

The video shows the machine in motion. The photograph shows the assembled build. The linked files let another maker inspect the available mounting and enclosure parts. Together they describe considerably more than a folder name.
