Keeping the study honest

I used this SketchUp model to work through a table with a dark top and two shaped wood supports. The scale figure helped show the proportions in the saved view. I do not have a finished build tied to this design, so I am presenting it as a furniture study. It still belongs in the portfolio because it shows how I was working through form and support geometry before committing material.
