One board, not the entire batch

A broad striped face and eased corners distinguish this piece. The original post describes the group as hybrid cutting boards; specific species are not assigned to this individual board from appearance alone.

The striped board from the batch

I designed and built this board from contrasting wood strips with rounded corners. It came from the March 2021 batch, but the wider stripe pattern makes it easy to separate from the tighter block layouts beside it. The work included the glue-up, flattening, edge treatment, and finishing needed to turn the pattern into a usable surface. I gave it its own page because the layout reads clearly enough to stand on its own.
