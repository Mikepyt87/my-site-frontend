An earlier printer setup

I brought the Ender 3 V2 into the shop in January 2021 and added a Raspberry Pi with OctoPrint the following month. I worked through the setup, connection, and calibration prints as I learned the machine. This page is useful because it shows the printer work before the later Voron and EffZero builds. It was a more basic system, but it taught me the setup and troubleshooting habits I carried into the next machines.

Credits & project notes +

Setup and prints: Michael Pytlowany. Creality printer, Raspberry Pi, OctoPrint, and existing calibration models are credited as platform/reference inputs.

The original adhesion observation remains a personal report, not evidence that OctoPrint alone caused a change.
