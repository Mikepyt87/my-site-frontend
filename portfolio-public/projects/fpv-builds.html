Learning the whole build

I started documenting these aircraft in November 2021 and kept going as the builds became more involved. The later projects included component choices, wiring, assembly, and configuration.

More than assembly

The hardware still had to work with the firmware and configuration. Power, compatibility, wiring, and packaging all mattered once the aircraft moved past the parts-on-a-bench stage.

Inside the LEGO-themed build

These July 2022 photos show the component layout and wiring inside the LEGO-themed aircraft. I linked the related build logs below for the full component details.

Learning the whole aircraft

I selected the components, assembled the aircraft, handled the wiring, and worked through the configuration. The LEGO-themed exterior is the part that stands out first, but the exposed photos show the hardware and connections underneath it. These builds taught me that an FPV aircraft is more than a frame with parts attached. Power, compatibility, firmware, layout, and setup all have to agree before the aircraft is ready to fly.

PLAYER1 / THE FRAME AND THE BUILD

A shared platform, a very different look.
