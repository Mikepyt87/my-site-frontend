Learn through the whole assembly

The build records start with a first documented aircraft in November 2021 and continue into experiments and configured builds in 2022. They capture component choices and reflections as the projects developed.

Beyond fastening parts together

The later records describe firmware and configuration work alongside assembly. Power, component compatibility and packaging all become part of bringing the aircraft together.

A closer look at the build

These photographs show the later LEGO-themed aircraft documented in July 2022. The exposed assembly makes the component layout and wiring visible. The related build logs are linked below.
