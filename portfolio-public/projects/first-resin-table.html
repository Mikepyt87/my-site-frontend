One from the beginning

This is the table I identified as my first wood-and-epoxy table when I revisited it in January 2021. The blue resin surrounds irregular wood sections in a circular top.

Looking back

In that later post, I could already see things I would make differently. It belongs here as part of the work, not just as a polished endpoint.

An early resin-table attempt

This was one of my early attempts at combining wood and blue epoxy in a table. The project came before the later river tables where I had more experience with layout, color, and finishing. I kept it in the portfolio because it shows the start of that learning curve. The work included preparing the wood, arranging the pour area, handling the resin, and bringing the result together as a finished table.
