One from the beginning

This is the table I identified as my first wood-and-epoxy table when I revisited it in January 2021. The blue resin surrounds irregular wood sections in a circular top.

Looking back

In that later post, I could already see things I would make differently. It belongs here as part of the work, not just as a polished endpoint.
