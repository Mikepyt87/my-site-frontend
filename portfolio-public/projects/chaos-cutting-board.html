My first chaos board

This was my first attempt at a chaos-style end-grain cutting board. I used cherry and walnut, broke up the regular block pattern, and worked through the glue-up without trying to make every section look predictable. The board was still meant to be used, so the pattern had to end in a flat, finished surface. I finished it with wood butter and kept the first attempt in the portfolio because it shows where that line of work started.
