A focused canvas

A set of coasters is a good-sized place to look closely at grain, color, and contrast. These pieces bring figured wood and resin together in a collection of functional objects.

The finish

I photographed this group after applying oil and wood butter. The finish brought out the figured grain and gave the coasters a surface ready for use.

Treating the coasters like finished work

I made this group from figured maple, oak, walnut, and resin, then finished the pieces with oil and wood butter. Coasters gave me room to try grain, color, and resin combinations without turning every experiment into a table. The size did not reduce the amount of care needed in the pour, surface cleanup, edge work, and finish. I kept them together as a collection because the different materials are easier to compare side by side.
