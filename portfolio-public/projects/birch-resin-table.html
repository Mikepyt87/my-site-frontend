Start with what is there

A box of short birch slats suggested this tabletop. Arranging the slats into a changing profile made the edge part of the design.

Wood and negative space

Resin fills the area beside the slats, letting the outline remain visible. The archive photographs show the components, clamping, and tabletop detail.
