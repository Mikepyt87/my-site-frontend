A distinct build

The post names the Player1 frame, FETtec 35A AIO, and Xing motors. The accompanying RotorBuilds record describes the experimental configuration.

Intent and operation are different records

The early build description was written before its maiden flight. It documents the assembled configuration and intended tradeoffs, not a measured flight benchmark.

Putting the 6S build together

I selected the Player1 frame, FETtec 35A AIO, Xing 2207 1855 KV motors, and the rest of the hardware for this 6S build. I handled the assembly and configuration and treated it as an experimental setup. The frame and component list document what I intended to build, while the available record does not prove every operating result. I kept the build details specific so the page shows the actual choices without turning an experiment into a performance claim.

FROM THE BUILD LOG

The Player1 platform, beyond one setup.

My March 2022 RotorBuilds entry records the move toward 6S, with cleaner packaging, strength and power-to-weight among the design goals. The later LEGO Edition uses the same Player1 platform: a BrockLeeFPV frame cut by WoodChuck FPV.

The model collection brings together the camera mounts, protection, chase-camera parts and skirt options around that platform.
