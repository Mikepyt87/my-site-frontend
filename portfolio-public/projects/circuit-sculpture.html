Follow a new direction

After seeing examples of circuit sculpture, I built one. This was a first exploration of a form that puts the connections on display.

The connections become the structure

Illuminated elements, exposed wiring, electrical components and a wooden base form the object. Instead of disappearing inside an enclosure, the connections become part of its visual character.

A place for exploration

This project sits at the intersection of functional construction and aesthetic composition. It extends the portfolio into a new material language through a made object.
