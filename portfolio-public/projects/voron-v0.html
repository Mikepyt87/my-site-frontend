Meet Nostalgia

This V0.2 is a different physical build from my EffZero. I also have two separate 250 mm Voron 2.4s; they are listed individually in the Tool Crib.

The June 2023 build record documents my Voron V0.2, named Nostalgia. The primary-color printed assembly gives this existing platform a distinctive personal identity.

The build continued after the first photograph

In November 2023 I documented fitting a Galileo 2 extruder. Because the printer used a Mini Stealthburner, the upgrade also used the standalone adaptation from hartk1213's repository.

Keep the inputs visible

This was an integration of existing designs, not an original extruder invention. The later upgrade adds a second chapter to the machine: choosing compatible community parts and adapting the build rather than leaving the first configuration untouched.

Credits & project notes +

Base platform: Voron Design. Galileo 2 extruder: JaredC01/Galileo2. Mini Stealthburner adaptation: hartk1213. Assembly and upgrade account: Michael Pytlowany.

The upgrade is described in the November post; the main photo remains the earlier June build record.
