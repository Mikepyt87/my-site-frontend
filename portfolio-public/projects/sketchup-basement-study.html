What survived with the model

This is an older SketchUp basement study, separate from the later Fusion bathroom work. The saved 256-pixel preview shows the interior walls, surfaces, and fixtures that were inside the original file. It is not a high-resolution render, but it is the real image that survived with the model. I would rather show that honestly than replace it with a new image that might suggest more detail than the original study contained.
