Turning the river idea into a tray

I used the wood-and-resin river idea in a rectangular serving tray instead of another table. The handles and low profile changed how the object needed to work, even though the material combination was familiar. I handled the design and fabrication, including the wood layout, gold-toned resin, and hardware. The original photo shows the tray beside the blue table pair, which makes the difference in purpose easy to see.
