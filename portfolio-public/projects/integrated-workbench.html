Building the bench around the work

I built this workbench to bring the table saw, laminated work surface, and woodworking vise into one working area. The layout was driven by how I expected to use the bench, not by making a standalone table and finding space for the tools later. It was already usable when I photographed it, although wheels, dog holes, and accessories were still planned. I am keeping those items listed as future work because the archive does not show them completed.
