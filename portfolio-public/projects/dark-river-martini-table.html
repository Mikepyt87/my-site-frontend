The darker version

This table uses a dark resin band between the two pieces of wood. It was photographed with another table, but this page shows the darker one on its own.

The darker table in the group

I designed and built this table with a dark resin band between the two pieces of wood. It was photographed with another table, but the darker pour and its metal base make it a separate piece. The layout is simple, which puts more attention on the grain, the natural edges, and the finish. This page gives that version its own place without pretending it was made as a completely separate collection.
