The challenge is the combination

A compact computer is not only a list of parts. The motherboard, graphics card, radiator, tubing, power supply, and cables all have to occupy the same limited enclosure. This build combines the supplied hardware in an NZXT H2 Flow Mini-ITX case.

The assembled exterior is deliberately only one view of the project. With the panels removed, the photographs make the internal packaging legible: the pump and memory on one side, the vertically installed graphics card on the other, and the radiator and cables running through the remaining space.

Show the inside, not only the finished lighting

The open-panel views are particularly useful here. They show the arrangement of commercial components and the routing that connects them, rather than hiding all of the integration behind the illuminated front.

The part choices include a modular SFX power supply and a 280 mm all-in-one liquid-cooling kit. Those details belong in the same story as the smaller motherboard and graphics card because they describe the actual assembled configuration.

A separate chapter from the first PC

The earlier 2021 custom PC is another chapter in the same collection. This newer build brings together the Core Ultra processor, RTX graphics, 64 GB of memory, two SSDs, and liquid cooling in a smaller-format system.

The full configuration is listed below. The exterior and open-panel photographs give the components a physical context, showing the computer as an assembly rather than only a specification sheet.
