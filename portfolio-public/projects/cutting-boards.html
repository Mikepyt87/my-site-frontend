The finishing stretch

The original post caught the batch at the end of finishing, while the remaining cotton rope was still on the way.

A collection with individual stories

The workshop produced more than variations on one pattern. The double-sided commission, the Lawson Twins, and the RV insert each have their own brief and construction details, so they also have individual project pages. This overview is the collection, not another count of those same builds.

Working through a full batch

This page brings together a batch of cutting boards made with mixed hardwoods, patterned glue-ups, and an oil finish. Each pattern required its own layout, but the shop work followed the same basic demands: solid glue joints, a flat surface, clean edges, and a finish suitable for use. I kept the group together because the photos show the range of patterns better than one board could. Several boards also have their own pages where the details are known.
