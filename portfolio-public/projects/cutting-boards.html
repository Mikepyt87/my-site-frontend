A batch, not just one

These hybrid end-grain boards were photographed together after oiling. The different arrangements make a small everyday object into an opportunity to try a different pattern.

The finishing stretch

The original post caught the batch at the end of finishing, while the remaining cotton rope was still on the way.

A collection with individual stories

The workshop produced more than variations on one pattern. The double-sided commission, the Lawson Twins, and the RV insert each have their own brief and construction details, so they also have individual project pages. This overview is the collection, not another count of those same builds.
