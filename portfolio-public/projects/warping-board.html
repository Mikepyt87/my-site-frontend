A tool with a theme

I made a 4.5-yard warping board in Michigan cherry and gave it a Star Trek theme, with an Enterprise motif and lettering inspired by Deep Space Nine.

From one kind of jig to another

The pegs organize yarn in preparation for weaving. At the time, I described it as the weaving equivalent of a woodworking jig: an unfamiliar use, but a familiar kind of making problem.

A working tool with a theme

I built this warping board from Michigan cherry and added the Star Trek-inspired carving because a shop tool does not have to look generic. The peg layout still had to work for winding yarn, so the theme could not interfere with the function. I handled the fabrication, CNC carving, assembly, and oil finish. The result is a practical weaving tool that also carries a personal reference in the design.
