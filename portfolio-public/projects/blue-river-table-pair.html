Two separate tables

I kept both tables together here because they were photographed and posted as a pair. The grain and resin paths make them easy to tell apart, even though I did not give them separate names in the original post.

Building the pair

I designed and built both blue-resin tables as part of the same July 2020 group. They use the same general wood-and-river idea, but the grain and resin paths make each top different. I kept them together on this page because that is how they were photographed and presented. The work included the wood layout, resin work, finishing, and fitting the tops to their metal bases.
