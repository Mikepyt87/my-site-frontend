Make another spot for plants

The shelves were intended to get plants off the ground and out of the cats' reach. Aromatic cedar and rope kept the construction simple and the wood prominent.

Two standard sizes, room for variation

The original offer listed two sizes: 18 by 10 inches and 21 by 8 inches. Custom dimensions and wood species were also available, with additional cost and lead time. That was a small product range, not only a single photographed shelf.
