Building my first custom PC

This was my first complete custom PC build. I assembled the system around an Intel Core i7-11700K, an ASUS Prime Z590-A board, an AORUS RTX 3070 Ti MASTER, and 32 GB of Corsair Dominator memory. Dan Slaby helped guide me through the build, and I handled the assembly and desk setup. The photos show both the hardware inside the case and the dual-monitor system in use.
