Make a new part meet an old one

The April 2021 account identifies a reverse-engineered jar lid with a 1/4-inch pitch. The photograph shows the blue prototype fitted to the jar, making the interface itself the focus of the project.

The blue prototype

I used edge-glow blue filament because it made the printed lid look like a three-dimensional blueprint. I left the prototype marking visible in the photo because that is the part I actually tested on the jar.

Starting with the thread

The jar already existed, so the lid had to begin with the interface instead of the outside shape. I measured and modeled the 1/4-inch thread pitch in Fusion, then printed a translucent-blue prototype to test the fit. The edge-glow filament made the part look like a three-dimensional blueprint, which suited the prototype stage. I left the marking visible in the photo because that is the actual lid I tested on the jar.
