Two halves of an idea

Birch slats and translucent resin form complementary tabletop shapes. The paired curves turn the paired side-table project into something more playful.

The table legs

A later post credits Scott at Scrappy's Crappy Art with making legs for one of the tables. The photos show more than one stage and base arrangement.

Making the two tops work together

I designed and built these birch-and-resin tabletops as a pair. The slats and translucent pour create complementary curved shapes, so each top makes more sense beside the other one. A later post credits Scott at Scrappy’s Crappy Art with making legs for one table, and the photos show more than one base arrangement. I keep that credit clear while focusing my part of the project on the tabletop design and fabrication.
