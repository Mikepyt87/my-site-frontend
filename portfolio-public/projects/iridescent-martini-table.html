A piece from the early series

The June 2020 photograph shows this table next to a separate dark-river version. Its shifting purple-blue color is the distinguishing feature.

The purple-and-blue version

I designed and built this martini table with purple and blue resin between two natural wood edges. The color shifts across the pour, which gives this one a different look from the darker table photographed with it. I kept the form simple and used a metal base so the top stayed the focus. This page covers the individual piece while the collection page shows how it fits with the other tables from that period.
