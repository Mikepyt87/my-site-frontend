Repair the fitting, keep the wardrobe

A cracked plastic joint left an otherwise usable wardrobe in need of one replacement component. I reverse-engineered the fitting in Fusion and printed a PETG replacement to meet the existing rails.

From the damaged part to the replacement

The corner fitting and latch are the model set for this repair. The project photographs show the damaged original and the purple replacement installed on the wardrobe rails.
