Player1

Camera mounts, protective parts, chase-camera setups and the skirt system, organized around the Player1 platform.

44 model files / 3MF / STL

Frame: BrockLeeFPV   /   Frame cutting: WoodChuck FPV

Parts around a platform

The Player1 set includes camera mounts, arm protection, chase-camera pieces and a modular skirt system. The skirt files cover different power-wire exits, capacitor arrangements and receiver/antenna configurations.

Moving to 6S

After losing an aircraft in Kauai, I planned a replacement around the Player1 frame and 6S power. My March 2022 build log sets out the priorities: a cleaner, stronger, simpler build with better power-to-weight.

The LEGO edition

The July 2022 build brought my interest in LEGO into a Player1 aircraft. BrockLeeFPV designed the frame, and WoodChuck FPV cut it. The build log follows the frame collaboration, component choices and assembly behind the colorful exterior.
